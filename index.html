About

I'm a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Prof. Sanghamitra Dutta. I started out building radar hardware that could read a hand gesture from a Doppler signature, and decoding intent from calcium-imaging traces — work where the signal is honest but the meaning has to be extracted carefully.

That habit of asking what does this signal actually mean has pulled me toward language models: why the same question asked two different ways can get two different answers, how to pass that judgment down to a smaller model through distillation, and how a language model trained on chemistry text reads a molecule differently than one trained on Wikipedia. I'm currently looking for research internships in NLP for Summer 2027.

Research

Selected

NLP

COFFEQA: counterfactual-guided few-shot distillation for LLM QA

A few-shot knowledge-distillation framework that pairs factual examples with counterfactuals to improve knowledge transfer in open-domain question answering. Introduces the Context Following Distillation (CFD) metric, which validates generated counterfactuals using teacher context-dependence and answer agreement. Evaluated across eight QA benchmarks with T5 and Qwen2.5 teacher-student pairs, including analysis of teacher-student agreement and distillation near the teacher's decision boundary. Under review, NeurIPS 2026

NLP

LLM consistency across paraphrased inputs

Probing classifiers locate the attention heads most responsible for paraphrase-induced inconsistency in LLM outputs; targeted activation edits at those heads reduce it. Evaluated with representational dissimilarity analysis on Gemma-2B-it over HotpotQA.

ML

A unified multimodal GNN-LLM framework for material property prediction

An embedding pipeline for biopolymer property prediction combining ChemBERTa-2 (structural, from SMILES) and MatSciBERT (semantic), via a substructure-to-text generation step that encodes per-component chemistry and inter-component interactions into natural language — improving on a GNN-only baseline. Currently exploring an AI-agent system to strengthen the framework's reasoning. Ongoing

Signal

Hand gesture recognition with Doppler radar

A vision-transformer / convolutional encoder-decoder architecture recognizing seven hand gestures in real time from a 24 GHz radar system, including the antenna array and beamforming hardware. IEEE Sensors Letters, 2022

More projects
ML

RNDC-Lab: real-time calcium imaging-based adaptive neural decoding

Integrating two prior lab systems (NDSEP, NeuroGRS) into one real-time, end-to-end decoding pipeline, tested on both simulated and real-world two-photon calcium imaging data. Frontiers in Neuroscience, 2026

ML

Dynamic pruning via dataflow parameterization

A parameterized channel-attention pruning method that compresses a model at inference time for resource-constrained deployment, tested on ResNet50 with CBAM on ImageNet.

NLP

Sentiment transition in updated Amazon reviews

Compared GPT-4, a BERT-based classifier, VADER, and fine-tuned DistilBERT on revised Amazon reviews across five product categories.

Signal

RF wireless sensing using micro-Doppler signatures

A 2.45 GHz ISM-band Doppler radar system with directional sensing, recognizing seven hand gestures in real time at over 94% accuracy — including the patch-antenna transmitter, a four-antenna receive array with Butler-matrix beamforming, and a convolutional encoder-decoder / transformer / MLP recognition pipeline. Undergraduate thesis work; a smaller set of related coursework projects (Tx/Rx systems, an FPGA multi-core processor, lossless compression) is in the CV.
